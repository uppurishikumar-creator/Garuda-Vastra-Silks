08 — Saree Care

Make your silk last for generations

Storing silk sarees

Wrap in a soft cotton or muslin cloth, never plastic. Refold along different lines every few months so the zari does not crack at the folds.

Washing & dry cleaning

Dry clean pure silks for the first few washes. For soft silks, a gentle cold-water hand wash with mild shampoo works — never wring.

Caring for zari

Keep perfume and deodorant away from the zari. Iron on the reverse side, with a cloth between the iron and the border.

Removing stains

Blot — do not rub. Bring the saree to us early; older stains are much harder to lift from silk.
