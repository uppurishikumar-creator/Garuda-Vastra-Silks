08 — Silk Preservation

Preserving Silk for Future Generations

Storing Pure Silk & Zari Sarees

Wrap in unbleached pure cotton or muslin fabric. Never store silks in plastic bags. Re-fold along different pleats every few months to prevent zari creasing and fiber stress.

Washing & Professional Cleaning

We strongly recommend dry cleaning pure Kanchipuram and Banarasi silks. For everyday soft silks, gentle cold-water wash with specialized mild shampoo is suitable. Never wring.

Protecting Genuine Zari Borders

Apply perfumes and hairsprays prior to wearing your saree to shield metallic zari from alcohol corrosion. Iron on the reverse side under a protective pressing cloth.

Spot and Stain Management

Blot immediately with a dry cloth — never rub vigorously. For ceremonial haldi or oil marks, bring the saree to our care team promptly for specialist treatment.
